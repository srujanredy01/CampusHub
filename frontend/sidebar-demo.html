Welcome back, Student

This is a demonstration of the redesigned CampusHub sidebar navigation. Try collapsing it, toggling dark mode, or resizing the browser to see responsive behavior.

Collapse Toggle

Click the panel icon in the sidebar header to collapse/expand.

Group Collapse

Click any section header (ACADEMICS, COMMUNITY, etc.) to collapse that group.

Dark Mode

Click the sun icon in the top bar to toggle between light and dark themes.

Responsive

Resize below 1024px to see the mobile drawer behavior with overlay.

Keyboard Navigation

Use Tab to focus items, Arrow keys to navigate, Enter to activate.

Tooltips

In collapsed mode, hover over icons to see tooltip labels.
